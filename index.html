TRAVEL THOUGHTFULLY

Good trips start
with good prep.

Travel is more rewarding when we're ready to be respectful guests. Keep these friendly reminders in your back pocket.

Before you go

Check current entry requirements, local advisories, insurance details and official travel guidance for your passport and route.

Tropical weather

Pack breathable layers, sun protection and a light rain shell. Check local forecasts and seasonal conditions before outdoor plans.

Getting around

Research local transport options, agree fares before a ride when appropriate, and use official transport information where available.

Culture with care

Dress respectfully at sacred sites, follow posted guidance, ask before photographing people, and learn a few local greetings.

Leave beaches better

Bring reusable essentials, keep a respectful distance from wildlife, and follow local guidance around reefs and marine areas.

Look after your things

Keep valuables secure, stay aware in busy places and save important documents somewhere separate from the originals.

For nature days

Check trail conditions, carry water, choose suitable footwear and follow park rules. Use a qualified local guide where required.

Eat your way, safely

Share allergies and dietary needs clearly, ask about ingredients, and choose food and water that feel right for your needs.

A friendly reminder: Details can change. Always verify entry rules, weather, local regulations and travel information with current official sources before departure.
